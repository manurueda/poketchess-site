Privacy Policy

PoketChess · get better at chess, five minutes a day

PoketChess collects very little. Playing works fully offline: there is no account, no sign-in, no advertising and no tracking across apps. Your games, lessons and progress stay on your device. When you are online, the app sends anonymous usage analytics that help us improve it, and buying PoketChess Pro is validated by our purchase provider. Both are explained below.

Purchases (PoketChess Pro)

Subscriptions and the lifetime unlock are sold and charged by Apple through your Apple ID; we never see your payment details. To confirm that you have Pro, the app sends the purchase receipt to RevenueCat, our purchase-validation provider, under a random anonymous identifier. RevenueCat keeps a record of the purchase (product, date, country, price) so it can restore it on your other devices. It is not linked to your name or email and is not used for tracking or advertising. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Usage analytics

We use Mixpanel to understand how PoketChess is used, so we can make lessons and puzzles better. The app tells Mixpanel which screens and steps you see (onboarding, lessons, puzzles, games, the Pro screen) and what happens in them (for example, a lesson finished with three stars), and it records your sessions in the app (session replay) so we can see where the app is confusing. PoketChess has no text fields, so these recordings only show the app's own screens: boards, buttons and Poke.

All of this is sent under a random identifier created when the app is installed, together with the device model, the iOS and app versions, and an approximate location (city and country) that Mixpanel works out from the connection. It is not linked to your name, email or Apple ID, it is not used for advertising or to track you across other apps and websites, and it is never sold.

Team notifications

A few moments (finishing the onboarding, seeing or closing the Pro screen, a purchase, finishing a lesson or a game) are also summarized in one short line that our server, hosted by Supabase, posts to a private Telegram chat of the PoketChess team. The line has no name or contact details: only the event, the app version and the last characters of the random identifier. Purchase events from RevenueCat reach that chat the same way.

Remote settings

The app downloads a short list of settings from our server (Supabase) to switch features on or off without an update. Nothing about you is stored for this.

What stays on your device

Your progress (lessons, stars, XP, streak, puzzle rating, achievements) and your settings are saved only on your iPhone or iPad, inside the app. Deleting the app deletes them. You can also reset your progress in Settings.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, it is scheduled locally by iOS on your device. No server is involved.

Children

PoketChess does not collect personal information such as names, email addresses or contact details from anyone, including children. The anonymous usage data described above contains none of them.

Third parties

Data leaves the device only to the providers named above: RevenueCat (purchases), Mixpanel (usage analytics), and Supabase and Telegram (team notifications). The app also bundles the open-source Stockfish chess engine and puzzles from the Lichess database (CC0); they run on your device and receive no data.

Your choices

Deleting the app stops all of the above. To ask us to delete the usage data sent from your device, write to us from the support page.

Changes and contact

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be published on this page. Questions: see the support page.
